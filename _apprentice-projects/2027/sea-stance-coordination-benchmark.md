A burst of similar social media posts can look like an organized campaign. In Southeast Asia, this judgment becomes harder when a message moves between languages, and even closely related languages. Many languages in Southeast Asia borrow from each other. This project develops multilingual models to tell these cases apart. Mentees will build an annotated benchmark of public social media conversations from Southeast Asia languages, and analyze their stance, similarity in languages, and also coordinating actors. This would involve multilingual fine tuning for semantic, linguistic and stance similarity analysis. It would also involve a graph network analysis of information diffusion and analysis.
