PH-LegalBench is an exciting opportunity to adapt the groundbreaking LegalBench project for the Philippine legal system. While LegalBench established a comprehensive evaluation suite for legal reasoning across multiple jurisdictions, this Philippine adaptation addresses the unique characteristics, complexities, and linguistic requirements of Philippine legislation. This project focuses on developing legal reasoning tests that reflect real-world Philippine legal scenarios. You'll work on creating evaluation tasks that assess AI's ability to interpret Philippine statutes, analyze local court decisions, and determine compliance with national regulations.

As a mentee, you'll contribute to building essential tools that will help advance legal technology in the Philippines, making legal services more accessible and accurate for everyone. This is your chance to work on cutting-edge AI research with direct impact on the Philippine justice system.
